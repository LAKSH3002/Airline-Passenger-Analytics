# Airline-Passenger-Analytics

Aim of Project: The aim of the project is to Analyze an Airlines Dataset and figure out passenger distribution & profile insight along with Operational profile Insights for that airline in specific airports, countries and Continents.

Dataset Details: Dataset has 98k rows and 14 columns. The columns are distributed as: First 6 columns represents passenger information and the rest displays Airport and departure details.

Problem statements solved in the project:
Q1 - What is the distribution of gender, nationality, flight status, airport continent and continent
Q2 - Which specific airport has most amount of delays for this flight? Take out top 10.
Q3 - Which airport had the maximum amount of on-time flights of this airline? Take out top 10
Q4 - Which airport had maxiumum amount of cancelled flights of this airline? Take out top 10
Q5 - Find out the number of passengers with an age greater than 30,40,50,60: Like give range and check.
Q6 - Country Name vs Flight delay status (Which country had the maximum amount of delayed flights) - Passenger Profile Insights
Q7 - Find out the airport with maximum flight delays and its percentage (Airport + delay percentage) - Operational profile insights
Q8 - For each continent calculate the percentage of delays, on-time flights and cancelled flights - Operational Profile insights
Q9 - What are the peak travel time of flights? - Operational Profile Insight
#     Month: 8, Year: 2022, Season: Summer
Q10 - From which country did maximum flights took off?
#      USA has the maxium flights taking off, followed by Australia, then Canada, then Brazil.
Q11 - How many pilots in total were there in this data?
#      There are 98610 total different pilots in the dataset.
